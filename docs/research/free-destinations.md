# Additional free destinations for developer and founder reach

Research snapshot: 2026-11-21. This extends [auth and access](auth-and-access.md), rather than repeating its destinations. Prices, queues, access and policies can change. **[UNVERIFIED]** means the primary sources checked did not establish a fact; it is not evidence that an API, free tier or restriction does not exist. Do not assume that a free submission guarantees publication or distribution. Provider pages are cited inline; platform policies and the actual community's current rules must be checked before every submission.

**Modes:** **(a)** API destination; **(b)** human handoff to an editor, compose or submission URL; **(c)** one-time project listing/launch, not a routine feature destination; **(d)** skip. A one-time listing can still use an API, but should not be modelled as a per-campaign post. Manual means the human uses the site, not automated browser scripting. For community and editorial submissions, default to human-written text; an unknown AI policy is not permission to submit generated prose. Do not prefill or copy generated prose to a destination that forbids it. Disclose affiliation, avoid duplicate submissions, and respect moderation and editorial decisions.

Effort and audience reach are **[JUDGEMENT]**, not measured time, traffic or guarantees: low = a few minutes after setup; medium = tailored submission and community context; high = significant new writing, assets or review. Categories below reflect discovery potential, not expected impressions.

## Ranked top 10 additions

**[JUDGEMENT]** Ranked for expected relevance and low cost for a developer/entrepreneur, not guaranteed reach. Conditional entries are explicitly not verified free or not universally appropriate. A per-feature entry means *selectively when the feature yields a genuinely relevant story*, never a blanket cross-post of every release.

1. **Lemmy programming communities — (b), selective per-feature/story.** Developer-specific discussion with no central gate; only after choosing a community and checking its rules.
2. **Console.dev — (b), selective per-feature/tool.** An official editorial contact and strong developer-tool fit; inclusion is discretionary.
3. **DevHunt — (c), one-time per project.** Free permanent listing targets developers; paid launch-week placement is separate.
4. **AlternativeTo — (c), one-time per project.** Durable discovery for eligible released software; free review may take a very long time.
5. **JavaScript Weekly / Golang Weekly — (b), selective per-feature/story.** One submission route, language-specific audiences; send only to the matching editor.
6. **Indie Hackers / Show IH — (b) selective story, (c) product profile.** Founder feedback is a strong fit, conditional on verifying current access and rules; avoid duplicate launches.
7. **Nostr — (a), per-feature social update.** Existing Crosspost integration keeps implementation modest for users already on Nostr; relay costs and reach vary.
8. **Uneed — (c), one-time per project.** Official free submission API, but free queue and publication thresholds make outcomes uncertain; choose manually and with explicit consent.
9. **Changelog News — (b), selective per-feature/news.** Direct editorial submission for qualifying open-source/developer news; commercial products and tutorials are excluded.
10. **freeCodeCamp News — (b), occasional human-authored tutorial.** Potentially broad developer education reach, but selective, high effort, and explicitly no AI-written text or art.

**Cadence boundary:** DevHunt, AlternativeTo, Uneed and other launch directories are one-time project records, not each-feature destinations. List PRs likewise add a project once per appropriate list. Lemmy, Nostr and editorial suggestions can recur only where the new content merits it. Blogging APIs are optional canonical-home/retention tools and should not be counted as new discovery by themselves.
